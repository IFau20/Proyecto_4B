# Catalog expansion — 15 November 2026

The catalog has expanded from 13 to **36 real notebook configurations**, identified by distinct manufacturer SKUs. The 23 additions represent ASUS, Lenovo, HP, Acer, and Gigabyte gaming and everyday laptops. All additional model identities, processors, memory and GPU fields were cross-checked against public product profiles from SoloTodo Chile. The profiles are retained individually in `backend/notebooks.ts`.

**No fixed prices were added.** The existing public-source feed attempts to find up-to-date retailer offers for each exact SKU, and the historical fallback is clearly labeled with its observation date when an active offer cannot be found. Only a listed, available store offer may be compared as an active listing. A product profile alone is not evidence of stock or a current price.

We reject refurbished offers and keep discount validation independent of card-only prices. Some SKUs may currently be unavailable (e.g. the older Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 variants); their historical last recorded price is not a quotation. Source: `https://www.solotodo.cl/products/<product-id>` per item in the catalog. Development assisted by ChatGPT/OpenAI.
